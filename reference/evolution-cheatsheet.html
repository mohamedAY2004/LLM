Distinctions people get wrong

  • Attention weights ≠ parameters. The scoring function is learned. The weights α are recomputed for every sentence and step.
  • Score vs weight. Score = before softmax (any real number). Weight = after softmax (≥ 0, sums to 1).
  • √dk keeps softmax out of saturation. Making the weights sum to 1 is softmax's job.
  • BERT's input embeddings are static. The context comes from the layers.
  • Self-attention is order-blind. Position must be added (sinusoids, learned embeddings, RoPE).
  • Masking means two different things: MLM [MASK] tokens (BERT's input) vs the causal attention mask (GPT's decoder).
